## Your client brief

**Your project brief is on TCU Online**, in the Content area, named for your team: `team-07-well-live.docx` is team 7's. It is the document your client wrote before the semester, and it is the only description of the problem you have until you meet them.

The briefs are not on this site. They are the clients' documents, not the course's, and they stay behind the TCU Online login.

## What happens next

Read your brief before Friday. [Friday's studio](studio.md) is your team's first hour together, and you spend it planning, fixing the weekly meeting time you will defend all term, and signing your [team contract](team-contract.md), not reading.
